Writing Approach Tutoring

Writing proceeds from the nature of our understanding.

What is the quality of our encounter with the world and reality in general? Good writing begins from the centeredness of our being, where we have a deep and clear experience of some aspect of truth. It is important to remember the fundamentals of the writing process. Our ideas, and the depth of our ideas, constitute quality writing. The format of the presentation is a secondary matter, though still significant.

Confusion can arise in the writing process because individuals may not understand that having a clear and independent understanding—an independent view of the world—permits them to communicate their experiences with momentum and flow.
