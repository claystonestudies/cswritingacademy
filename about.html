Claystone Studies Writing Academy aims to contribute to the capacity of individuals to discover and communicate ideas.

About Claystone Studies Writing Academy

Claystone Studies Writing Academy is the writing and communication portion of Claystone Studies. Claystone Studies was started to contribute to a community space of learning, knowledge sharing, and intellectual discovery. At the Writing Academy, information and tutoring are offered to help individuals improve in their writing and communication skills.

About Robert Davis

Robert Davis is an educator and a perpetual student of knowledge, interested in entering deeper into the wonders of the universe through the paths of knowledge, understanding, and dialogue. Writing tutoring is among the services Robert offers. The craft of writing and language has been important in the journey and path of education in his life. Robert received a B.A. in Philosophy from Cornell University, where he graduated with Distinction in All Subjects. Robert is interested in questions about how knowledge is shared in a community, what the ultimate nature of knowledge is, and how we, as a human community, enter into deeper levels of knowledge. In his tutoring, Robert seeks to promote the student's ability to enter into deeper perceptions of truth and reality and communicate those perceptions in a satisfactory manner.
