Tutoring

Writing tutoring sessions are on Zoom with Robert Davis. After you order, you can schedule on Calendly.

Booking

Tutoring sessions with Robert are organized around the approach, understanding, starting points, and learning style of the student. The overarching goal of writing tutoring is to contribute to your position as a writer to successfully communicate your thoughts and perceptions from a place of solidity, clarity, and knowledge. There are many practices and methods that can lead to the attainment of this goal. Robert values the approach of proceeding slowly, smoothly, and reasonably in order to balance steadiness and depth in the learning process. A writing tutoring session with Robert is an opportunity to explore more deeply your own potentiality and capacities for unique expression.
