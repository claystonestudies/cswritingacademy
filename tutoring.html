Writing Tutoring with Robert Davis

What is a writing tutoring session with Robert like?

Tutoring sessions with Robert are organized around the unique approach, understanding, starting points, and learning style of the individual. The overarching goal of writing tutoring is to contribute to your position, as a writer, to successfully communicate your thoughts and perceptions from a place of solidity, clarity, and knowledge. There are many practices and methods that can lead to the attainment of this goal. Robert values the approach of proceeding slowly, smoothly, and reasonably in order to balance steadiness and depth in the learning process. A writing tutoring session with Robert is an opportunity to explore more deeply your own potentiality and capacities for unique expression. As philosophical knowledge and inquiry are also part of the knowledge base of Robert, writing tutoring sessions can include philosophy and major critical thinking work.
