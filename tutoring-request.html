Tutoring Request

Share a little about what you are looking for before booking a session.

← Back to Tutoring

Please address the points below in an email to Robert. You may write briefly or in detail, whichever is most useful.

  1. 1

    What kind of writing, reading, critical-thinking, speech, or debate support are you looking for?

  2. 2

    What would you like to accomplish through tutoring?

  3. 3

    How often would you be interested in meeting, and what days or times generally work for you?

  4. 4

    Please include any context that may be useful: starting points, educational background, current work, interests, strengths, or challenges. If the request concerns a student, you may also include their grade level.

Robert reviews tutoring requests for fit and availability. If the tutoring is appropriate and a time is available, he will reply with next steps and booking instructions.
