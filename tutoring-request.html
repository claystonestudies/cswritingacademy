Tutoring Request

← Back to Tutoring

You can submit a tutoring request to Robert, containing any questions you may have or detailed preferences you would like to confirm. The three points below may be useful guiding points for the message of your tutoring request.

  1. 1

    What kind of writing tutoring—potentially including reading, critical-thinking, speech, philosophy, or debate support—are you looking for?

  2. 2

    What would you like to accomplish in writing tutoring?

  3. 3

    You can include any context that may be useful: starting points, educational background, current work, interests, strengths, questions, or challenges. If the request concerns a student, you may also include their grade level.

Robert reviews particular tutoring requests for fit. If the tutoring is appropriate, he will reply with next steps and booking instructions.
